Privacy Policy

This interim notice explains how the current Intoday landing page handles information. It will be replaced or expanded before additional account or workspace features are made publicly available.

Last updated: August 10, 2026

1. Information you provide

The current landing page does not contain a registration form, payment form, newsletter form, or embedded contact form. If you contact Intoday by email, we receive the email address, message, and any information you choose to include.

2. Information stored on your device

The landing page stores your selected display language in your browser’s local storage. This preference remains on your device so the page can remember your language after a refresh. The page does not use this preference to identify you.

3. How information is used

Information sent by email is used to respond to your enquiry, provide requested information, and maintain necessary correspondence. We do not sell personal information received through the contact address.

4. External services and links

The site links to X and may open your email application when you select Contact. Those third-party services process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Intoday does not control their practices.

5. Data retention and protection

Email correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose of the conversation, record-keeping, or applicable legal requirements. Reasonable measures should be used to protect information, but no electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices

You may clear the saved language preference through your browser settings. You may also contact us to ask about personal information you previously provided by email, including requests for correction or deletion where applicable.

7. Changes to this notice

This notice may change as Intoday develops. Material updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy may be sent to contact.intoday@gmail.com.

This is an interim notice for the current landing page, not a representation of data practices for unreleased product features. It should be reviewed before public production use.
